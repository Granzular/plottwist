What to include in a report

  • Bugs: the steps you took, what you expected, and what happened. Include your browser and device if the interface is involved.
  • Translation problems: a small MATLAB example, the generated Python, and any translation warnings. Describe the expected mathematical behavior.
  • Execution failures: the MATLAB source, generated Python, and the error shown in the command window.
  • Documentation errors: the page or guide section and the correction you suggest.
  • General feedback: the practical or task you were working on and what would make the workflow clearer.

Remove credentials, personal information, and code you do not have permission to share before sending a report. Download your source while the result is still available.
